# Credits & Asset Attribution

## Original Lab Concept
- Characters (Mây, Bóng Mực), world (Đảo Đèn Lồng, Đêm 1 – Đảo Bến), and gameplay requirements are original materials of Day 12 AI SDLC Lab.

## 3D Models, Materials & Effects
- All 3D geometry (island terrain, stone lantern pedestals, Great Lanterns, Mây with conical hat and mini-lantern, fireflies/motes, ink Shades, drifting sea of clouds, starfield) is **100% procedurally generated in code** using Three.js primitives and custom canvas/shader materials. No external 3D models or textures are used.

## Audio
- All sound effects (mote pickup chime, lantern ignition chord, pulse wave, shade hit thud, win/loss jingles) are **100% procedurally synthesized in-browser** using the Web Audio API (`src/render/audio.js`). No external audio samples are used.
